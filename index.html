About This Space

You hold the
answers
within you.

Intuition Earth Ancestors Somatic Breathwork Yoga

Pula Wellness Space is an initiative founded by Pheidah Ngoepe — a black South African woman, medical doctor, occupational therapist, and certified yoga teacher (200-hour Yoga Teacher Training) — whose own journey through yoga, meditation, breathwork, somatic practices, and journaling has shaped a deep conviction: that well-being is accessible, and that the path to it lives within each person.

This space is not about prescribing answers. It is about creating the conditions for you to access the wisdom that already lives in your body, your mind, and your being. It is about helping you move through the world feeling grounded, supported, and authentically yourself.

The framework here is rooted in connecting to the elements of the earth, to spirituality, to spiritual guides — ancestors, angels, nature — and to the truth that people are agents of their own lives. The support you need already exists: within you, and around you.
