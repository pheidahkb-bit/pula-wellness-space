About This Space

You hold the
answers
within you.

Intuition Pleasure Joy Somatic Breathwork Yoga

Pula Wellness Space is a relational and growth space created by Pheidah - a black South African human and wellness practitioner - whose own journey through practices including yoga, meditation, breathwork, somatic practices, and reflective journaling has shaped a deep conviction: that well-being is accessible, and that the path to it lives within each person. Their knowledge and skill background in the wellness space include training as a vinyasa yoga teacher and work as an intuitive/tarot reader, medical doctor and occupational therapist.

This space is not about prescribing answers. It is about creating the conditions for you to access the wisdom that already lives in your body, your mind, and your being. It is about helping you move through the world feeling grounded, supported, and authentically yourself.

The framework here is rooted in capacitating your nervous system through breath and movement practices and resourcing through connection to elements of nature and your spirit guides. The support you need already exists: within you, and around you.
