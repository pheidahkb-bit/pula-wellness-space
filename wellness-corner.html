Pula Wellness Space Podcast

Conversations about well-being — what it means, how we access it, and what it looks like to move through the world feeling grounded, supported and authentically yourself.

A space for honest, pluralistic conversations about well-being — what it means, how we access it, and what it looks like to move through the world feeling grounded, supported and authentically yourself. Hosted by Pheidah Ngoepe — wellness practitioner, yoga teacher, intuitive reader, medical doctor and occupational therapist — this podcast draws from a wide range of knowledge traditions, both ancient and contemporary, to explore what it truly means to be well. Come as you are.
Episode 01
Go itheeletša — The art of tuning in

In this first episode we explore the Sepedi concept of Go itheeletša — taking the time to be present, to connect to your senses, and to tune into the wisdom that lives in your body. Through the lens of this beautiful concept from the Sepedi language, we explore why presence and sensory awareness are not just practices of mindfulness but a crucial entry point into understanding and accessing your own well-being.
